# **Privacy Policy for Quowally**

**Effective Date:** 22\09\2025

Quowally (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use Quowally.

---

## 1. **Information We Collect**

Quowally is a Quote Wallpaper App. We **do not collect personal information** such as your name, email, or location.

The app may access your device storage **only to save wallpapers** if you choose to set or save them. This is necessary for the app to function properly.

---

## 2. **How We Use Information**

Since we do not collect personal data, your privacy is respected. The app uses access to your device storage **only to allow saving and setting wallpapers**.

---

## 3. **Third-Party Services**

Quowally may use the following libraries/plugins to improve functionality:

* `path_provider` – Access storage to save wallpapers.
* `share_plus` – Optional sharing of wallpapers via other apps.
* `workmanager` – Background tasks (no personal data collected).

These third-party services **do not collect personal data** through our app.
